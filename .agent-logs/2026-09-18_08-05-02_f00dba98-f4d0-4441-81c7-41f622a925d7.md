## How Ask beats Fathom's version

- **Hybrid retrieval:** vector search plus Postgres full-text search, with the two rankings fused. Names and numbers are exactly where pure embeddings miss.
- **Scope control:** all meetings, this meeting, or a filtered set by person or date.
- **Inline `[n]` citations:** each renders as a chip showing meeting title, speaker and timestamp. Hovering shows the exact quote, and clicking opens `/meetings/:id?t=…`, which scrolls to the segment, highlights it and seeks the audio.
- **Server-side citation validation:** any `[n]` that does not map to a retrieved chunk is stripped. If retrieval comes back empty, the answer is "not found in your meetings", not a guess.
- **Cross-meeting synthesis:** the seed meetings will be interrelated: one fictional company, with a decision that changes across three calls. A question like "how did the launch date change?" then has a real multi-meeting answer.
